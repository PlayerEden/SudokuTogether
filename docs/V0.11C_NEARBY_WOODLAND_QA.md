# GridMates v0.11C — Nearby play and Woodland QA

Verified November 4, 2026 (America/Chicago). This is the next isolated QA milestone after the owner-accepted v0.11B. [Draft PR28](https://github.com/PlayerEden/SudokuTogether/pull/28) targets `feat/gridmates-coach-launcher`; main/development are not promoted.

## What changed

New nearby rooms default open. A partner taps the named room to join; hosts can opt into Require a code, and that choice survives Home navigation and subsequent new rooms. Resuming restores the saved room's actual policy, including legacy protected rooms. Occupied/reserved partners and rejoin tokens remain protected. Manual addresses stay behind Join manually. Missing discovery access flags default protected.

Multiplayer has a stable primary title row and a secondary Chat/Game details row. Occasional room controls live in a scrollable dialog. Chat identifies local messages as You, retains the partner's chosen name, wraps quick replies and keeps typing usable with larger text.

The optional Woodland collection adds12 packaged portraits: owl/fox starters, then rabbit1, bear2, badger3, hedgehog4, robin5, deer6, squirrel8, otter10, tortoise12 and cat15 distinct completed puzzles. Assisted Solo/Daily/co-op completions and versus wins count. The six earlier emblems and their unlock rules remain. Choosing an emblem clears the portrait; unknown or locked portraits restore safely.
