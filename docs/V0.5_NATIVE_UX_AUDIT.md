# Sudoku Together — v0.5 native UX quality audit

Branch: `polish/v0.5-native-ux` (stacked on the v0.4 QA readiness branch).

## Product intent

This is a **native Android Kotlin/Jetpack Compose** app, not a WebView, browser-based HTML game, or embedded site. Visual direction: reserved cream and forest, considered typography, clear Sudoku-first interaction, native sheets and confirmations, light emphasis, no unnecessary decorative cards or effects.

## Source UX issues discovered and resolved

1. **Competing home CTAs.** Previous home displayed a long list of equal-weight buttons for all three modes. Replaced with Solo / Co-op / Versus selection and context-appropriate host/join/resume actions. Name and cosmetics are grouped into a discrete player section, not competing with start game.
2. **Rules hard to discover.** Added native "How to play" from home and "?" during the puzzle. Explains 1–9 row, column, 3×3 rules, candidate/pencil marks, Erase and Undo, and multiplayer conditions. Dialog scrolls for large fonts.
3. **Puzzle selection guidance.** A selected square now lightly highlights its row, column, and 3×3 region without displacing the strong selected-square, conflict, matching-digit, or partner highlight.
4. **Overweight player chrome.** Replaced two comparatively tall multiplayer identity cards with a concise one-row strip while retaining emblems, names, and player ink.
5. **Mode ambiguity.** Notes now visibly reads "Notes ON" with a distinct outline/fill when active; Erase, Undo and Notes preserve minimum 48dp height. The active home mode is exposed through screen-reader semantics.
6. **Dead end after completion.** A finished game no longer leaves a disabled number keypad onscreen. A dedicated result panel offers Play Another to solo/host players, and a clear next-game hint to guests.
7. **Low-contrast small secondary copy.** Darkened Muted from #6D7C73 to #55665C (approximately 4.0:1 to 5.55:1 against cream). Gold small-label color changed from #C39443 to #8A6729 (approximately 2.5:1 to 4.71:1 against cream). This particularly helps dense mobile layouts.

## Intentional constraints / remaining QA

- 9×9 touch cells can be smaller than 48dp on compact displays; the density is inherent to the puzzle grid. Selection descriptions and feedback mitigate but do not replace a real TalkBack/small-phone test.
- The entire screen may scroll on short devices and when keyboard/font scaling is extreme. Confirm number entry remains convenient with a real screen.
- QA debug apps have the `.qa` ID and are signed with a test key. Upgrading between independent GitHub runners may require uninstalling the prior QA app; do not erase personal achievements without confirming and do not treat QA saves as cloud-backed.
- Test dark board with unusual ink colors and TalkBack / high font scales.
- System haptics and music/audio are not added in this pass; introduce only if they improve measured usability rather than for decoration.
