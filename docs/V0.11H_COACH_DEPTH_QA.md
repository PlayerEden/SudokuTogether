## Try the Coach

- Open **Hint → Show why**. Hidden pairs show the two candidates to keep and cross off the extras. X-Wings explain the four supporting squares in either orientation. [Hidden pairs](https://hodoku.sourceforge.net/en/tech_hidden.php#h2) · [X-Wings](https://hodoku.sourceforge.net/en/tech_fishb.php)
- **Next step** takes you through the candidate changes. At a placement, **Go to square** selects the real cell and closes the preview. You enter the digit.
- If the deduction path stalls on a board whose solution is verified unique, the Coach offers **Reveal number**. The digit appears only after that tap. A request to the Coach counts as one hint, including the reveal preview. Reopening a recent unchanged position in the same session preserves the count. **Check my work** remains the path for a conflicting or incorrect entry.

The reveal search stops after 200,000 nodes. Only a completed uniqueness check authorizes a reveal. The app reads earlier saves and uses nearby protocol version 7.
