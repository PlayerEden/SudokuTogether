## Decisions and limits

- First contained stage only; coach proof/apply, result sharing, profile/cards and calibrated scoring follow separately. Cost: those features are planned rather than bundled into this APK.
- Separate defaulted check counter preserves logical-hint meaning. Cost: both local result paths carry additional compatible metadata.
- Hint smoke checks use the actual explanation, unchanged cells and saved hint count. Cost: editorial copy alone is not their contract.
- Primary owns real screenshot inspection and final installed identity/update proof. Cost: emulator captures do not establish every OEM/device or TalkBack behavior.
- Physical two-phone LAN, newer Android, tablets and TalkBack remain open/deferred. The inherited extreme-font active-board/Medium/landscape refinements remain outside this contained coach/result acceptance. No release certification.
- Carried Play/AdMob policy guidance is planning and must be rechecked against official requirements at release. No publication or monetization was performed.
- No measured solver latency failure justified an engine change; worst-case performance is not established here.
- Requested checks may focus their suspect cell; selection clears feedback and explicit Erase uses the existing selected-cell edit. Cost: focus can move after a check request.
- Existing warned-backup recovery can restore an older validated snapshot after destruction of the newest primary. Latest metadata is not guaranteed under arbitrary file loss. Direct nonzero-check recovery and checked-Daily/immediate-prune combinations remain narrower untested combinations; existing recovery/pruning suites pass.
- Intentional proposal Markdown hard breaks remain. Source comparison uses tracked path/mode/type/blob hashes rather than treating that formatting as a behavioral failure.
- Test-only Dismiss cleanup does not certify every physical keyboard/back/IME path. Sharing tests intercept the chooser and do not send the APK to another person.
- **Deferred Minor:** carried GRIDMATES_BRAND_AND_SERIES working-source/implementation metadata conflicts with its correct current-baseline preamble. This handoff, delivery plan and current QA/source pointers take precedence. Provisional connected-tiles art is unchanged.
