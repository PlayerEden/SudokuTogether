## Player-facing changes

The existing MainActivity has a separate launcher label **Sudoku**, so the home screen/app drawer does not truncate the full name. **GridMates: Sudoku** stays the game brand; the QA application label retains its QA suffix. Package, Activity component, three icon resources, signing identity and private-file paths remain intact.

Solo and Daily offer **Check my work** inside the Hint dialog, including when no supported logical step is available. It explicitly compares entered values against the original puzzle's unique solution. It identifies one wrong entry without giving its replacement or changing the board; **Erase entry** is a separate player action using normal Undo. Correct entries, repeated numbers and an empty board receive truthful short feedback. Existing logical hints remain deductions from the visible grid.

Solution comparisons are saved before feedback appears and tracked separately from logical hints. A checked puzzle is Assisted even after Undo/resume; both Solo and Daily finish/history keep the count. Repeated-number and empty checks do not add assistance. Failed saves and superseded sessions reveal no unrecorded result. Checks remain unavailable in co-op/versus. Older records default to zero checks and retain their earlier meaning. Schema1/protocol5, min26/target35 and the original solving/generation engine remain unchanged.

Version18 / `0.11.0-dev2-qa`. No new dependency, artwork change, release signing, Play publication, remote play or numeric score. Later stages are planned in the [delivery plan](GRIDMATES_DELIVERY_PLAN.md).
