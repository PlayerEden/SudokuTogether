Help, live game status, save-error/destructive warnings, deduction explanations and assisted/unassisted facts remain.

There is no engine, storage, recovery, LAN or profile-schema change. Package `com.playereden.sudokutogether.qa`, schema1, protocol5, min26/target35 remain. Version17 / `0.11.0-dev1-qa`. No new UI/font dependency, repository rename, Play publication, cloud/profile expansion or final art lock.
